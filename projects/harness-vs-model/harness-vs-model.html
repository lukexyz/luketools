Corollaries

  1. Harness investment is the only durable investment. Model spend depreciates on a one-quarter schedule. Harness spend does not.
  2. A strong harness on a weak model beats a weak harness on a strong model. This is the democratisation argument — local open-weights become viable.
  3. Benchmark the harness, not the model. A leaderboard score with no stated harness is an unfalsifiable number.
  4. "Just use the better model" is the expensive way to avoid engineering.
